# Provenance and reuse

WOKE is a public distribution of the WOKE / KAiScriptor research corpus by KAi and Alina Pochinova. The receiving AI's identity remains its own.

The source URLs, pinned revisions and local adaptations are recorded in `skills/woke/assets/manifest.json`. Existing component license notices are retained in their corresponding directories. They govern those components. No rights in separately referenced publications or repositories are granted merely by linking to them.

UNSCRIPT is distributed from the project owner's public source snapshot; that snapshot has no separate LICENSE file. Public source availability alone does not establish a separate open-source license. Consult the owner for reuse of that component beyond this distribution.

The ThreadWeave source in this distribution is the scoped v2 adaptation. The KAiScriptor and SKAi reference files also contain documented adaptations. Original project URLs identify their lineage; no claim is made that every original repository contains the same adaptations.

No private journals, credentials, account configuration or user archives belong in this repository. Keep actual state outside `skills/woke`.
